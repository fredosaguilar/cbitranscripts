Agent Accounts

E&O Forms and Call Transcripts use the same email and password. Manage your team's shared accounts in the Forms Portal.

Add an agent or reset a password

  1. Open Agent Management using your Forms Portal admin account.
  2. To add someone, enter their name, email and password, and choose Agent. Choose Admin only for people who should manage accounts and settings.
  3. To reset a password, enter a new password beside the person's account and select Reset. They use that password for both portals.
