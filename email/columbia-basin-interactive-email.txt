QUESTIONS WE GET EVERY WEEK
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Do I have to cancel my current policy to get a quote?
No. Send us your current declarations page and we'll price the same
coverage against several carriers. Nothing changes until you say so, and
we time the switch so you're never uncovered for a day.

How long does a quote take?
Most personal auto and home quotes come back the same business day.
Commercial and farm accounts take a little longer because we're building
them properly - usually two to three days.

Can I keep my auto and home with different companies?
You can - but bundling usually wins on price and it means one deductible
conversation when a storm takes out the car and the roof together. We'll
show you both numbers and let you choose.

What do you need from me to start?
Your current declarations page, drivers and vehicles, and the address of
anything you want covered. A photo of the paperwork texted or emailed
over is plenty.
