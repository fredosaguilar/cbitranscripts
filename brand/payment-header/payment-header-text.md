# Simply Easier Payments — payment header text

Two header slots, each a plain-text block shown at the top of the pages and
receipts you tick on the grid below the input box. Plain text only — no
formatting, so these read as sentences rather than bullet lists.

The two slots do different jobs: one is read **before** money moves, the other
**after**. The first is where the protective wording belongs, because a
disclaimer nobody read before paying is worth much less than one they did.

---

## Header 1 — before payment

Tick this for every payment type, on the payment entry / details step.

**Short version**

```
Columbia Basin Insurance · 509-765-8839 · 21 D St SW, Suite A, Quincy, WA 98848.
A payment made here is applied to your policy. It does not by itself reinstate
cancelled coverage, bind new coverage, or change an existing policy. If your
policy has cancelled or you are paying to stop a cancellation, call us at
509-765-8839 to confirm. Please allow 1-2 business days for posting.
```

**Fuller version**, if the box takes it

```
Columbia Basin Insurance · 509-765-8839 · 21 D St SW, Suite A, Quincy, WA 98848.

A payment made here is applied to your policy. It does not by itself reinstate
cancelled coverage, bind new coverage, add or remove a vehicle, driver, or
property, or change any limit or deductible. Those changes are only in effect
once we confirm them to you in writing.

If your policy has already cancelled, or you are paying to stop a cancellation
that is pending, call 509-765-8839 before you pay. Reinstatement is the
carrier's decision, not ours, and paying here does not guarantee it.

Please allow 1-2 business days for the payment to post. Office hours are
Monday to Friday.
```

## Header 2 — receipt and confirmation

Tick this for every payment type, on the confirmation / receipt step.

```
Thank you. This confirms Columbia Basin Insurance received your payment. It is
a receipt for the amount paid — it is not confirmation that your policy is
active, has been reinstated, or has been changed. Keep it for your records.
Questions about your coverage: 509-765-8839 or fred@columbiabasininsurance.com.
```

## Why the wording is shaped this way

The exposure a payment page creates is a client who pays and reasonably
concludes they are covered. That belief is most dangerous exactly when it is
most likely: a cancelled policy, a payment made the day before a loss.

So the wording separates two things people naturally merge — *we received your
money* and *you have coverage* — and says the first without implying the second.
It also sends anyone in the risky situation to the phone before they pay, which
is the only step that actually resolves it.

It deliberately does not say anything about how payments are processed or
secured. That is Simply Easier Payments' claim to make, not the agency's.

**Have your E&O carrier or agency counsel read this before it goes live.** It is
customer-facing language about what coverage is and is not in force, written to
be defensible, but it is not legal advice and the carrier may want its own
wording.
